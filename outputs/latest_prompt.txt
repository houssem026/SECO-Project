Architectural visualization of a future-facing civic institution, showcasing a complete facade redesign while strictly preserving the original building's exact camera angle, perspective, massing, footprint, and structural floor rhythm. 

The building is wrapped in a highly innovative, double-skin facade. The outer layer consists of a self-supporting, sculptural geometric lattice made from light ivory Ultra-High Performance Concrete (UHPC) with a smooth, matte acid-etched finish. This porous ivory screen features a parametric gradient of varying aperture sizes, transitioning seamlessly into softly curved, solid Glass Fiber Reinforced Concrete (GFRC) panels that wrap around the building’s corners. 

The inner layer is a high-performance, dark-tinted glass curtain wall recessed one meter behind the lattice, creating deep, dramatic three-dimensional shadows and a rich sense of depth. The sub-structure is entirely concealed to maintain a seamless, monolithic appearance. The original building's scale, site context, and floor levels remain unchanged. Elegant daytime architectural lighting casts soft, clean shadows across the ivory concrete, emphasizing its organic, flowing geometry. Clean, crisp, realistic, and highly detailed.
